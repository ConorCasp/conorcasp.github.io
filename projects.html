My Projects

Project 1

Cycling Wayfinding Application

I am currently working on developing a cycling wayfinding application for the Eau Claire, WI metropolitan area. The goal of this project is to make it easier for people to navigate the area by bike while also encouraging local tourism and outdoor recreation.

Through this project, I am gaining experience with research, mapping, and GIS while learning more about how transportation and technology can be used to support local communities. I hope to continue developing my GIS skills and use what I learn from this project in future work related to community development, planning, and geographic data.
